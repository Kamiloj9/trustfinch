# Contributor Code of Conduct

Trustfinch welcomes respectful, constructive participation from everyone. Be professional, assume good intent, and focus criticism on ideas and changes rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be handled discreetly and without retaliation.
